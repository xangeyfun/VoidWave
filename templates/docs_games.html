Every game runs in an interactive embed with buttons, so you play by pressing them rather than typing commands. Most games work in servers, DMs, and private channels. Games against other people start with a challenge the opponent accepts.

Solo games

you against the bot
/8ball servers, DMs and private channels

Ask the magic 8-ball anything and get a random answer in a styled embed.

Parameters
question Your question. Required.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.
/hangman servers, DMs and private channels

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before you run out of attempts. Letters are picked with the buttons on the embed.

/wordle servers, DMs and private channels

Guess the 5 letter word in 6 tries. Feedback colors tell you which letters are right, right but misplaced, or wrong, standard Wordle style.

/minesweeper servers, DMs and private channels

Clear the minefield without hitting a bomb. The grid is 20 cells, and you can choose how dense the mines are.

Parameters
mines Mine density as a percentage. Defaults to 25 percent (5 mines on the 20 cell grid). Optional.
/battleship servers, DMs and private channels

Sink the enemy fleet with salvos across a grid before it sinks yours.

/15puzzle servers, DMs and private channels

Slide the tiles into order to solve the classic 15 puzzle. Moves are counted, so try to finish in as few as you can.

Multiplayer games

with friends, or the bot

For the head to head games, leave the opponent option empty to play against VoidWave, or pass a member to send them a challenge. The challenge must be accepted before the match starts.

/rps servers, DMs and private channels

Rock, paper, scissors. Pick your move with a button and see who wins.

Parameters
opponent A member to play against. Leave empty to play the bot. Optional.
/tictactoe servers, DMs and private channels

Classic 3 by 3 tic-tac-toe on a button board.

Parameters
opponent A member to challenge. Leave empty to play the bot. Optional.
/connectfour servers, DMs and private channels

Drop your pieces into the grid and line up four to win.

Parameters
opponent A member to challenge. Leave empty to play the bot. Optional.
/blackjack servers, DMs and private channels

Play blackjack against the dealer, or start a lobby where up to 4 players join and play a hand together.

Parameters
max_players How many players can join the table, from 2 to 4. Defaults to 4. Optional.
/trivia-battle servers, DMs and private channels

A lobby based trivia showdown where players answer questions each round to rack up points and claim the win.

Parameters
category Question topic: General Knowledge, Video Games, Music, Science & Nature, Computers, Geography, History, Animals, Films, Books, or Any. Defaults to General Knowledge.
difficulty Any, Easy, Medium, or Hard. Defaults to Easy.
rounds From 1 to 10, defaults to 5.
max_players From 2 to 8, defaults to 4.
answer_time Seconds per round, from 10 to 60. Defaults to 20.
