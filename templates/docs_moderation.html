All moderation commands work only in servers and require both you and the bot to hold the matching permission. Hierarchy checks prevent you from acting on the server owner, the bot itself, or anyone whose highest role is equal to or above yours (or the bot's).

Member actions

kick ban timeout unban
/moderation kick kick members

Remove a member from the server. They can rejoin if they have an invite.

Parameters
member The member to kick. Required.
reason Reason for the kick. Optional.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.
/moderation ban ban members

Ban a member from the server. Optionally delete their recent messages from the last 0 to 7 days.

Parameters
member The member to ban. Required.
delete_days Days of messages to delete, 0 to 7. Defaults to 0. Optional.
reason Reason for the ban. Optional.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.
/moderation unban ban members

Unban a user by their Discord ID. Works even if the user is not in the server.

Parameters
user The user to unban. Required.
reason Reason for the unban. Optional.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.
/moderation timeout moderate members

Temporarily prevent a member from sending messages, reacting, or joining voice. Duration is set with an amount and unit (minutes, hours, days). Maximum is 28 days.

Parameters
member The member to timeout. Required.
amount How long the timeout lasts. Required.
unit minutes, hours, or days. Defaults to minutes. Optional.
reason Reason for the timeout. Optional.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.

Channel controls

slowmode lock unlock
/moderation slowmode manage channels

Set or clear slowmode on a channel. Value is in seconds, 0 clears it, maximum is 21600 (6 hours).

Parameters
seconds Slowmode delay in seconds. 0 clears. Required.
channel Channel to change. Defaults to the current channel. Optional.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.
/moderation lock manage channels

Prevent members from sending messages in a channel by denying Send Messages for @everyone. The bot keeps existing permission overwrites intact.

Parameters
channel Channel to lock. Defaults to the current channel. Optional.
reason Reason for locking. Optional.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.
/moderation unlock manage channels

Remove the Send Messages deny for @everyone on a previously locked channel, restoring normal permissions. If the channel was not locked, the command tells you.

Parameters
channel Channel to unlock. Defaults to the current channel. Optional.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.

Role management

add remove
/moderation role add manage roles

Give a role to a member. The role must be below both your highest role and the bot's highest role, and cannot be @everyone.

Parameters
member The member to give the role to. Required.
role The role to add. Required.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.
/moderation role remove manage roles

Remove a role from a member. Same hierarchy restrictions apply. If the member does not have the role, the command reports that.

Parameters
member The member to remove the role from. Required.
role The role to remove. Required.
hidden Show the result only to you. Optional.

Hierarchy protection

safety first

Before any action the bot verifies:

Check What it prevents
Server owner You cannot kick, ban, timeout, or role manage the server owner.
Bot itself You cannot act on the bot.
Bot top role Target's highest role must be below the bot's highest role.
Your top role Target's highest role must be below your highest role (unless you are the target).
Role is @everyone Cannot add or remove the @everyone role.

If any check fails, the command responds with a clear message explaining why.
