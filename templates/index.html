What it does

Everything your server needs

Leveling System

Earn XP from messages and voice chat. Unlock level roles and climb the server leaderboard.

/level • /leaderboard • /profile

AI Chat

Chat with a self-hosted Llama 3.2 model. Your chats are never sent to third-party AI services.

/ai • @VoidWave

Question of the Day

Daily prompts posted on schedule, with auto-generated threads, role pings, and timezone support.

/config qotd

Moderation

Kick, ban, timeout, lock channels, set slowmode, and manage roles with built-in permission checks.

/moderation kick • /moderation timeout • /moderation lock

Fun Commands

Calculator, animal pics, quotes, coin flips, random numbers, and more.

/calc • /animal • /quote

Utilities

User info, ping, uptime, and quick tools for keeping tabs on your bot.

/userinfo • /ping • /uptime

Games

Play tic-tac-toe, connect four, hangman, blackjack, trivia, and wordle right in your server.

/tictactoe • /trivia • /wordle

Vote Rewards

Vote on top.gg, discordlist.gg, or discordbotlist.com to earn 2x XP boosts, with optional reminders right in your DMs.

/vote • /vote-remind

Web Profiles

Every member gets a shareable web profile with their stats, rank, and level progress.

/level • web profile link
