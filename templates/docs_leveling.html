VoidWave tracks leveling per server. Every server runs its own leaderboard, and your total across all servers is tracked as a global profile. XP comes from chatting and from hanging out in voice channels.

How XP works

messages and voice

Voting doubles your XP. Vote with /vote to get a 2x XP boost for 4 hours, or 6 hours on weekends. The boost applies to both message and voice XP. See Voting and boosts for the full picture.

Source XP Rules
Messages 1 to 15 XP The message must be at least 5 characters, and you can earn message XP at most once every 30 seconds in the same server. Message count is always recorded, even when XP is on cooldown.
Voice 1 to 8 XP Rewarded roughly every 10 minutes. At least 2 non bot members must be in the channel, and you must not be self deafened. Time is always counted; XP only kicks in when those conditions hold.

Levels and rank titles

ascend the tiers

Your level rises from total XP. Progress to your next level is shown as a bar on your stats (progress / out_of XP). The amount needed grows with your level.

Along the way you earn cosmetic rank titles. Level 1 is Stardust, and every milestone level upgrades your title and its color. Reaching a milestone is announced as a Rank Up instead of a regular level up.

Commands

check your stats
/level server only

Shows your level, rank in the server, global rank, total XP, progress to the next level, message and voice stats, an active vote boost if any, and the next level reward role if one is set.

Parameters
user View another member's level card instead of your own. Optional.
hidden Send the result only to you instead of the channel. Optional.
/leaderboard server only

Opens the interactive leaderboard for the server. Page through entries with the arrow buttons, jump to your own rank, and toggle between server, global, and combined views right from the embed. The same leaderboard is available on the website at /leaderboard.

Sort options
Level Ranked by level, with XP as the tiebreaker. The default.
Total XP Ranked by lifetime XP.
Total Messages Ranked by how many messages each member has sent.
Total Voice Ranked by total time spent in voice channels.
Voters Ranked by most recent vote. Shows when each person last voted for the bot.
Parameters
sort Which statistic to rank by, from the list above. Optional.
global_lb Show the global leaderboard across all servers instead of this one. Optional.
combined Merge your stats across every server into a single row, then rank globally. Not available for the voters list. Optional.
hidden Show the leaderboard only to you. Optional. Note the embed still counts as interactive for the person who ran it.
/profile servers, DMs and private channels

Your global profile, combining total XP, messages, and voice time across every server VoidWave tracks you in.

Parameters
user View another member's global profile. Optional.
hidden Send the result only to you. Optional.

Level rewards

roles for milestones

Admins only. The commands in this section require the Administrator permission in your server.

Admins can tie roles to levels. When a member reaches a configured level, VoidWave grants the matching role automatically. Each level can only hold one role, so pick levels that make sense for your server.

/config level add_role administrator

Give a role at a specific level. The bot needs the Manage Roles permission, and the role must sit below the bot's highest role in the server settings so it can be assigned.

Parameters
level The level at which the role is granted.
role The role to grant.
/config level remove_role administrator

Remove a level reward. The level picker autocompletes from levels that already have a role assigned.

Parameters
level The level whose reward role should be removed.

Admin setup

announce level ups

Admins only. These commands require the Administrator permission. The fastest path is /config auto, which creates and enables a voidwave-level-ups channel for you and turns on a daily question channel too. Full details are in Configuration.

/config level set_channel administrator

Pick the channel where level up and rank up announcements are posted. The bot checks that it can view and send messages in the channel first.

Parameters
channel The text channel for announcements.
/config level toggle_channel administrator

Turn level up announcements on or off. You must set a channel first. The bot verifies it can view the channel, send messages, and embed links before enabling.

Parameters
enabled True to announce level ups, False to stay quiet.
/config level toggle_vote_announce administrator

Also announce when a member votes for the bot, posted to the same level up channel when enabled. Both this and the channel toggle must be on for announcements to show.

Parameters
enabled True to announce votes, False to skip them.
