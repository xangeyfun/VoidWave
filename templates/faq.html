Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VoidWave.

Still stuck? Join the support server

What is VoidWave?

VoidWave is a free, open-source Discord bot focused on leveling and engagement. It tracks XP, messages, and voice time, rewards members with level roles, posts a Question of the Day, and includes moderation, AI chat, music, and fun commands. You can invite it with the Invite button in the top bar.

How does XP and leveling work?

Messages earn a small random amount of XP (1-15 XP, once every 30 seconds, and only for messages at least 5 characters long), and spending time in voice channels earns voice XP. XP builds toward your level, and once you fill your progress bar you level up (and can earn a level role if the server has configured them). You can check your progress with the /level command.

How do level roles work?

Server admins can configure roles that are granted automatically at specific levels (for example, a role at level 5, another at level 10). Whenever you level up, the bot adds the role for your new level. Setting these up is covered in the Setup Guide.

How does voice XP work?

You earn a small amount of voice XP at most once every 10 minutes, as long as at least one other person is also in the voice channel with you (your /vote XP boost applies here too). Being in a channel alone or self-deafened does not earn XP. Your total voice time is tracked either way.

What is Question of the Day (QOTD)?

QOTD posts a daily prompt from a static list of questions into a configured channel, opens a thread for members to answer (auto-archived after 24 hours), and pings a role. It requires no user data - it just sends a pre-written question. Admins enable it with the /config qotd command, and by default it posts at 4:00 PM server time.

Does VoidWave use AI chat?

Yes. Use the /ai command, mention @VoidWave, or reply to one of its messages. The LLM chat feature runs on a self-hosted Llama 3.2 model, so prompts and replies never leave VoidWave's own server and are never shared with third-party AI services. Conversations are stateless, so no history is kept between messages. AI replies are opt in: turn them off for yourself with /aitoggle, or have an admin disable them for the whole server with /config ai toggle. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can VoidWave play music?

Yes. Join a voice channel and run /music play with a link or search term. VoidWave builds a queue and supports pause, resume, skip (by vote), shuffle, loop, volume, and disconnect. See the commands page for the full list of music commands.

What data does VoidWave collect?

The bare minimum to work: your user ID, guild ID, username/display name, avatar hash, XP, level, message count, and voice time. Message content and AI prompts only appear in server logs for debugging and are never stored in the database. We never sell or share your data. Read the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

How do I request data deletion?

Message our support server or DM xangey and we'll remove your data. Removing the bot from a server also stops all tracking for that server.

How do I invite VoidWave?

Click the Invite button in the top bar and follow the Discord authorization flow, or use the invite on the bot's top.gg page.

How can I support VoidWave?

Vote for the bot on top.gg, discordlist.gg, or discordbotlist.com (each site has its own cooldown) to earn a 2x XP boost for 2 hours, or 3 hours on weekends. Use /vote-remind to get a DM when you can vote again. You can also join the support server or contribute on GitHub - VoidWave is open source.
