Every vote for VoidWave grants a 2x XP boost that applies everywhere, in every server, to both message and voice XP. The boost is tied to your user, not to a single server, so it follows you around. You can vote on Top.gg and Discord List, both give the same reward, and the boosts stack.

🗳️ Vote on Top.gg 🗳️ Vote on Discord List

How the boost works

vote, then earn double
What Value
Boost 2x XP on messages and voice for the whole duration.
Duration 4 hours per vote (6 hours on a weekend on Top.gg, where weekend votes carry double weight). Votes stack: voting on both lists in one round adds both durations, up to 10 hours on a Top.gg weekend.
Registration Voting through any of the links registers automatically via webhook. You get a thanks for voting DM right after. If the other list is still ready, the DM includes a button to vote there too and stack another 4 hours.
Cooldown Each list allows one vote per user every 12 hours. The lists keep separate timers, and /vote shows when each one is ready again.

Keep the streak going. After a vote, /vote-remind syncs a reminder to the soonest list that frees up, so you are pinged right when you can vote again. The reminder DM shows the timer for each list and puts a vote button on every list that is ready. Every reminder also has a Turn off reminders button, so you do not need the command to opt out.

If you have a streak of 2 or more days and have not voted by late in the UTC day, the same opt-in sends one streak at risk DM with a vote button, so a missed day does not reset it by surprise.

Commands

vote and reminders
/vote servers, DMs and private channels

Shows the vote links for both lists as clickable buttons with each list's own cooldown timer, plus a button to the monthly voters board. It also shows whether your 2x XP boost is active and for how long, your lifetime votes, badge, progress to the next badge, streak and best streak, and whether a reminder is set. The message is only visible to you.

/vote-remind servers, DMs and private channels

Turns DM reminders on or off. When you vote, the reminder re-syncs to the soonest list that frees up. Each list has its own 12 hour cooldown, so you may get pinged once per list per round. When a list is already ready, the reminder fires within about a minute. Reminders also include the late-day streak at risk ping.

Parameters
enabled True or False to set it explicitly. Leave it out to flip the current state.
/uptime servers, DMs and private channels

Shows the bot uptime and lists the official links, including the vote pages on Top.gg and Discord List.

Top voters

badges, streaks, monthly board

The vote page is one place to vote and to see who is winning: both vote buttons at the top, then a streak leaders strip and the monthly board with a podium for the top 3, badges and streaks. The board pages 25 voters at a time and the month picker shows previous months, with the current month marked. Use the find field to locate any voter and jump straight to their rank. The Discord leaderboard also has a Voters sort that ranks members by votes this month (UTC). The regular website leaderboard ranks by level, total XP, messages and voice time.

Badges and streaks

show off your support

Voting also earns bragging rights, tracked per user and shown on your profile, on /vote, and on the monthly board. Your profile and /vote also show how many votes are left until your next badge.

Reward How to earn it
🥉 Bronze Voter 5 lifetime votes.
🥈 Silver Voter 25 lifetime votes.
🥇 Gold Voter 100 lifetime votes.
🔥 Voting streak Vote on any list on consecutive days (UTC days). Your current streak and your best streak show on /vote and your profile. With reminders on, a late-day ping warns you before a streak resets.

Badges are cosmetic. They never change your XP. The XP reward is always the stacking 2x boost.

Server announcements

admins only

Admins only. Vote announcements require the Administrator permission.

Admins can announce member votes in the level up channel. This nudges the whole server to vote, since each vote shows the voting member and their new 2x XP boost. Each announcement has a vote button for both lists, plus a pointer to /vote and the vote page.

/config level toggle_vote_announce administrator

Enables or disables vote announcements. They are posted to your level up channel, so that channel must be set with /config level set_channel and enabled with /config level toggle_channel.

Parameters
enabled True to announce votes, False to turn announcements off.
