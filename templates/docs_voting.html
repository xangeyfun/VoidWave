Every vote for VoidWave on Top.gg grants a 2x XP boost that applies everywhere, in every server, to both message and voice XP. The boost is tied to your user, not to a single server, so it follows you around.

How the boost works

vote, then earn double
What Value
Boost 2x XP on messages and voice for the whole duration.
Duration 4 hours on a weekday, 6 hours on a weekend. Weekend votes on Top.gg carry double weight, which extends the boost.
Registration Voting through the Top.gg link registers automatically via webhook. You get a thanks for voting DM right after.
Cooldown Top.gg allows one boost per user every 12 hours.

Keep the streak going. After a vote, /vote-remind syncs a reminder to 12 hours after your most recent vote, so you are pinged right when you can vote again. You never have to remember the cooldown yourself.

Commands

vote and reminders
/vote servers, DMs and private channels

Shows the Top.gg vote link, whether your 2x XP boost is currently active and for how long, and whether a reminder is set. The message is only visible to you.

/vote-remind servers, DMs and private channels

Turns DM reminders on or off. When you vote, the reminder re-syncs to your most recent vote, 12 hours ahead. When the cooldown is already over, the reminder fires within about a minute.

Parameters
enabled True or False to set it explicitly. Leave it out to flip the current state.
/uptime servers, DMs and private channels

Shows the bot uptime and lists the official links, including the vote pages on Top.gg, Discord List, and DiscordBotList.

Voters leaderboard

who voted, when

The leaderboard has a Voters sort that lists members by when they last voted, most recent first. It works in the Discord command and on the website leaderboard. Through the command, scroll to the voters sort with the sort option.

Server announcements

admins only

Admins only. Vote announcements require the Administrator permission.

Admins can announce member votes in the level up channel. This nudges the whole server to vote, since each vote shows the voting member and their new 2x XP boost.

/config level toggle_vote_announce administrator

Enables or disables vote announcements. They are posted to your level up channel, so that channel must be set with /config level set_channel and enabled with /config level toggle_channel.

Parameters
enabled True to announce votes, False to turn announcements off.
